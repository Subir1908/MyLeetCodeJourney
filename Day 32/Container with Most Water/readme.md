Solved the Container with Most Water probleme where we have to find the optimal heights which can contain most amount of water based on area rather than volume as it is in 2D.
Used two pointer approach for this by taking the widest container first by two farthest heights.
The final algoritm has a time complexity of O(N) and space complexity of O(1).
It has a runtime of 0ms and 62.97 MB memory.
