Solved the Climbing Stairs problem where we can only take 1 or 2 steps at a time to reach n steps of a staircase.
Took me a while to understand that it follows the fibonacci sequence. Implemented the method to calculate the fibonacci sequence and it got through.
The final algorithm has a time complexity of O(N) and a space complexity of O(1).
